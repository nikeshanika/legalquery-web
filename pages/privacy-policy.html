6. Data Storage and Security

At LegalQuery.in, the security of your personal and sensitive data is a top priority. We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, misuse, loss, or alteration—whether during transmission or while at rest. These security measures are designed in accordance with industry best practices and applicable Indian data protection regulations.

We store your data on secure servers hosted in data centres that meet international standards for data security and compliance. To further ensure data integrity and confidentiality, we use encrypted channels and secure protocols whenever data is exchanged between users, lawyers, and the platform.

Some of the key security practices we follow include:

  • Use of Secure Servers and Encryption: All communications between your browser and our servers are protected by Secure Socket Layer (SSL)/Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocols (i.e., HTTPS), helping to ensure that data transmitted is encrypted and protected from interception.
  • Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC): Access to user data is granted only to authorised personnel, based on their job responsibilities. Lawyers, support staff, and internal teams have limited, permission-based access to ensure data is only available to those who need it.
  • Regular Security Audits and Monitoring: We routinely monitor our systems for vulnerabilities and conduct periodic security audits to detect and address any potential threats or weaknesses.
  • Restricted Access to Legal Content: Any sensitive legal documents, contracts, or personal case-related materials uploaded by users are accessible only to the Lawyer(s) involved in the matter and authorised support staff, strictly on a need-to-know basis.
  • Data Minimisation and Retention Practices: We collect and retain only the data necessary for service delivery and compliance purposes. Data is stored for as long as required to fulfil contractual obligations or comply with legal retention mandates, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

While no system can guarantee complete security, we are committed to continuously enhancing our security posture. Users are also encouraged to adopt good security practices such as using strong passwords, logging out after use, and reporting any suspicious activity promptly.

If you believe that your data has been compromised or you have concerns about the security of your account, please contact our support team immediately at support@legalquery.in.
